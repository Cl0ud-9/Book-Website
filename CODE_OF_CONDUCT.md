# Code of conduct

The Bookshelf is a small project. Be kind, be specific, and assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, not with people.

- Describe problems in a way the other person can act on: "the shelf overlaps the text at 375 px, here is a
  screenshot" helps; "the layout is broken" does not.
- Answer questions without making anyone feel small for asking, and credit other people's work and ideas.
- Insults, harassment, discrimination, sexualised language, publishing someone's private information, spam and
  pressure on the maintainer are not acceptable.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block people who
keep doing so. To report behaviour that worries you, contact the maintainer privately through the address on their
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/Cl0ud-9), or through the
[security form](https://github.com/Cl0ud-9/Book-Website/security/advisories/new), saying it is a conduct report.
Reports are handled quietly, and the person who reports is not named.
